Make the device list the product. Use direct rules instead of abstract roles: Always mute for internal speakers, Never mute for the DJM. Blue belongs only to the selected output; muted safety stays neutral unless it fails.

“Working” has honest layers

Connected means CoreAudio reports the device alive. Active means it is the default output. I/O running means a client is using it. A signal meter can prove digital samples are flowing—but no software can prove the mixer, cable, and PA are audible without hardware feedback.

Three cues, three meanings

Blue row + check means the selected output. A slashed speaker means held muted. Amber or red means attention is required. Device rules are written as actions—Always mute or Never mute—rather than color-coded roles.

Product rules

Three rules keep the utility honest.

01 · PER DEVICE

The rule follows the UID

Remember Always mute or Never mute by stable CoreAudio device UID—not by whichever output happens to be default.

02 · NEVER TOUCH DJ

“Never mute” means read-only

The app may report connection, routing, format, I/O, and signal. It never writes mute or volume to the DJM.

03 · SAFE FALLBACK

“Always mute” is continuous

Keep internal speakers muted even when inactive, across wake and reconnect, so an output failover cannot create an audible burst.

Scope recommendation

Ship routing confidence and safe fallback together.

Version 1

  • Live list of connected output devices
  • Always mute / Never mute rule stored by device UID
  • Default-output, device-alive, I/O-running, and sample-rate status
  • Continuous verified mute for Always mute devices
  • Warning when the expected DJM disappears

Optional or later

  • Process-tap signal meter
  • Changing the default output from the menu
  • Volume slider or writes to Never mute devices
  • Automated test tones—they are unsafe at a live gig

The device list earns its space. It is not merely a macOS output picker: it shows the selected route, explicit app behavior, connection health, and mute status that Control Center does not.
