The selected route owns the strongest cue.

Blue row and white-ringed disc: the DJM is the output; the shield says the app never touches it. The speakers stay grey — mute glyph, padlock — and need no more attention than that.

Menu bar · Icon + Route

Speaker glyph plus a three-letter route tag (DJM / INT). For a DJ, where audio goes is the glanceable fact; colour carries state. Narrow, mono, unambiguous.

The spec line

The bus pill (USB 2.0, BT 5.2 — nothing for built-in) sits inline after the device name. The meta line is format then channels — 24bit 48khz – 12 in, 10 out — no glyphs, so nothing clashes with the mute speaker on the slider below. The Style menu item picks the tray icon style, mirroring the picker above.

Mute lives on the slider

Every volume bar starts with a small speaker — the anatomy of the macOS Sound slider. A slashed speaker + dimmed bar is mute; clicking that icon toggles it. It sits exactly where your eye goes to read the level, instead of hiding in the spec line.

Locks

Every row trails a circular lock button — the disk widget's eject anatomy. Open padlock on translucent grey = unlocked; filled white padlock = Always mute; blue shield = Never mute (read-only). A mute-locked device also gets a dashed border; the active output stays clean-edged unless it is itself locked.
